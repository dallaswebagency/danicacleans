FAQ

Quick answers before you book. Anything else, just send me a message.

Ask a question
Do you clean Airbnbs or short-term rentals?

Yes, I clean Airbnbs and other short-term rentals.

Do you do move out cleans?

Most of the time no. But I will occasionally do move out cleans for current clients if they move while I'm already cleaning for them.

Do you have a minimum number of hours?

Yes. Within Sherwood Park it's 2 hours. Edmonton is 3 or 4 (east or west of the river, generally). St. Albert is 4. Spruce Grove is 5. For other areas, please contact me.

Will you clean my business while it's open?

No. The odd worker popping in quickly while I clean on a weekend is fine, but I don't clean while a business is generally open to the public or to staff. Coworking spaces with 24/7 member access are an exception.

Can I provide supplies for you to use?

No. I only use my own cleaning supplies, including cleaning products, vacuum, rags and mop. No exceptions. I'm more than happy to accommodate eco-friendly cleaning requests.

Are you insured?

Yes. I carry liability insurance as well as WCB.
